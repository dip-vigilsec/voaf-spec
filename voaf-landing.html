Why VOAF?

Tamper-Evident Hash Chain

Every entry includes a SHA-256 hash of the previous entry. Break one link, the whole chain screams. Append-only by design.

Provider Agnostic

Works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Cohere, open-source models — any HTTP-based AI API. No vendor lock-in.

Gate Decisions are First-Class

ALLOW, BLOCK, and FLAG decisions are built into the schema. Blocked requests stay in the audit trail — you see what was stopped and why.

Minimal by Default

Only 8 required fields per entry. Everything else — session tracking, decomposition, token counts — is optional. Start simple, extend as needed.

Privacy-Preserving

Store content hashes without raw content. Prove what happened without exposing what was said. Full content is always optional.

Machine & Human Readable

JSON schema with formal validation. Pipe it into your SIEM, render it in a dashboard, or read it in a text editor.
